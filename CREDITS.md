# Instruments (instruments/)

Sampled instruments for **Heresy** in Castle Danger Studio. Each folder holds one instrument: `instrument.json` (which file plays which notes and velocities, plus loop points) and its MP3 samples. `index.json` lists them all.

All samples come from **VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition** by Versilian Studios (recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell; organ by Simon Dalzell of Ivy Audio), released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain): https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE

Changes made here: converted to MP3, leading silence trimmed, and seamless loops baked into the sustained instruments. Credit to the original authors and Versilian Studios is appreciated but not required.

# Percussion (percussion/)

Sampled drums for **Ossuary** in Castle Danger Studio, also from VSCO 2 CE (CC0 1.0). Converted to mono 16-bit WAV (WAV keeps drum hits exactly on time; MP3 adds a short silence at the start), trimmed, and the softer velocity layers raised in level so they sit closer to the loud ones.

| File | VSCO 2 CE source |
| --- | --- |
| snare-hit-v1…v9-rr1/rr2.wav | Percussion/Snare2-HitSN (snares on), velocity layers 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, two round robins |
| snare-roll-f.wav | Percussion/Snare2-rollSN_v5 (+8 dB) |
| snare-roll-mf.wav | Percussion/Snare2-rollSN_v3 (+8 dB) |
